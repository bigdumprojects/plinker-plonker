How it works
Hit numbered pegs to score. A peg gives its current value, then halves. Empty pegs disappear. Beat quota to restock and earn a coin.
Board rules
21 peg slots • powers of two • one merge pass on restock • full-board clear doubles the round score.
Prototype items
Pants = every 10 pops, pop a random peg for double points.
Cactus = every 5 pops, bonus random peg pop.
Double Trouble = 25% chance each launch to double launch score.
Controls
Mouse only. The board is built with Canvas, so it runs directly in a browser without a framework.
